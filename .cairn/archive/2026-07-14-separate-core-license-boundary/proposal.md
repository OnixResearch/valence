## Why

Valence's canonical Evidence IR is an interoperability layer intended for embedding across the Onix stack and external evidence producers. Applying AGPL to `valence-core` makes every linked consumer confront strong combined-work licensing, while the Valence CLI and executable pilots can retain AGPL reciprocity.

## What Changes

- License `valence-core` under MPL-2.0.
- Keep `valence-cli`, executable pilots, and repository application surfaces under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
- Add complete MPL and AGPL texts and an explicit package map.
- Preserve canonical evidence identity and third-party terms.

## Impact

Evidence IR modifications remain file-level copyleft, while larger producers and consumers may retain their own licenses. CLI/application changes remain AGPL.
